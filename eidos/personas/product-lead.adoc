== Profile
The Product Lead is responsible for product strategy, customer outcomes, capability definitions, and feature scoping. In Eidos, they express business needs as living capabilities and Jobs-to-be-Done, collaborating seamlessly with architects, engineers, and autonomous AI agents through machine-validated Git workflows.
