= Capability: Product Exploration & Hypothesis Spikes
:toc: left

== Job To Be Done (JTBD)
Investigate early ideas, technical feasibility, and architectural benchmarks in sandboxed spikes before committing to formal specifications.

== Intent
Allows product managers and engineers to conduct high-uncertainty research without being burdened by strict acceptance criteria prematurely. When an exploration validates its hypothesis, it graduates into a formal `SpecDelta`. If discarded or adopted, older terminal explorations beyond the `keep_recent` window are partitioned and slimmed into `eidos/explorations/archive/` (`== Core Hypothesis` and `== Findings & Learnings`) via `eidos delta archive` while preserving `graduated_from` and `decision.resulting_deltas` traceability.
