= Component: DeltaCommands
:toc: left

== Responsibility
Implements the `eidos delta` subcommand group (`status`, `list`, `show`, `create`, `verify`, `conflicts`, `audit`, `apply`, `delete`, `archive`, `changelog`) for managing and inspecting the SpecDelta (`DLT-*`) architectural mutation lifecycle.

== Key Responsibilities & Collaborators
* **SpecDelta Inspection & Lifecycle Orchestration**: Routes `--path` discovery across all `eidos delta` subcommands through `require_cli_eidos_dir` (`CMP-CLI-APP`, auto-routing path-shaped `show`/`verify`/`apply`/`audit`/`status`/`delete` targets to their owning monorepo sub-project and failing closed with `discovered_subprojects` hints at rootless monorepos), exposes `delta_show_cmd` (`eidos delta show`) to inspect active (`eidos/deltas/`) and archived (`eidos/deltas/archive/`) SpecDeltas in Rich or `--json` format while preserving the disjoint `spec`/`delta` boundary, exposes `delta_conflicts_cmd` (`eidos delta conflicts`) to detect parallel target and 1-hop blast-radius overlaps across open SpecDeltas (`INV-DELTA-PARALLEL-CONFLICT-DETECTION`), and delegates blueprint creation, status transitions, mutation verification, in-place application, deletion, and two-tier archive slimming to `CMP-DELTA-ENGINE`.
* **Deletion Auditing & Changelog Generation**: Delegates reverse-dependency leak auditing (`eidos delta audit`) to `CMP-DELTA-AUDITOR` and Keep a Changelog generation (`eidos delta changelog`) to `CMP-CHANGELOG-GENERATOR`.

== Public Surface
* `delta_app: typer.Typer`
* `delta_status_cmd(...)`, `delta_list_cmd(...)`, `delta_show_cmd(...)`, `delta_create_cmd(...)`, `delta_verify_cmd(...)`, `delta_conflicts_cmd(...)`, `delta_audit_cmd(...)`, `delta_apply_cmd(...)`, `delta_delete_cmd(...)`, `delta_archive_cmd(...)`, `delta_changelog_cmd(...)`

== Design Constraints & Operational Notes
* **Verify-Gated Application**: `eidos delta apply` runs post-condition verification by default (`--verify/--no-verify`) and exits with code `1` if any declared mutation is unverified.
