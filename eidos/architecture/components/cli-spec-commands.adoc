= Component: SpecCommands
:toc: left

== Responsibility
Implements `eidos validate` and the `eidos spec` subcommand group (`list`, `show`, `schema`, `create`, `delete`) for inspecting, validating, scaffolding, and deleting living catalog specifications across all 15 non-delta specification types.

== Key Responsibilities & Collaborators
* **Root Validation (`eidos validate`)**: Resolves `--path` through `resolve_cli_eidos_dir(path)` (`CMP-CLI-APP`), invokes `CMP-SPEC-VALIDATOR` (`SpecValidator.validate_directory`), and renders Rich validation tables or compact `orjson` JSON reports (`--json`, listing only failing documents in `documents` alongside `schema_errors` and `broken_references`).
* **Living Catalog Management (`eidos spec`)**: Routes `--path` discovery across `list`, `show`, `create`, and `delete` through `resolve_cli_eidos_dir(path)` and delegates operations to `CMP-LIFECYCLE-SCAFFOLDER` while enforcing a strict disjoint boundary from `eidos delta`.

== Public Surface
* `register(app: typer.Typer) -> None`, `spec_app: typer.Typer`
* `validate(path: Path, json_output: bool) -> None`
* `spec_list_cmd(...)`, `spec_show_cmd(...)`, `spec_schema_cmd(...)`, `spec_create_cmd(...)`, `spec_delete_cmd(...)`

== Design Constraints & Operational Notes
* **Disjoint Delta Rejection**: Rejects `delta/mutation` (`DLT-*`) in `eidos spec list --type delta`, `show` (directing callers specifically to `eidos delta show <ID>`), `create`, and `delete`, keeping `eidos spec` and `eidos delta` cleanly separated.
