:toc: left

== 1. View Purpose & Specification Inventory
The Catalog View (`SCR-PORTAL-CATALOG`) provides a global, searchable inventory of every living specification document in the repository:
* **Global Search**: Substring matching across document ID, title, schema, and relative file path.
* **Type Filtering**: Quick filters by business (DOM, CAP, PER, POL), design (DS, UIC, SCR), tech (CMP, CTR, ARCH), and delta (DLT).
* **Document Inspection**: Clickable rows opening full specification YAML frontmatter and AsciiDoc content in a detail modal.
