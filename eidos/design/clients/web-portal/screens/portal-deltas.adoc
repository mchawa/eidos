== 1. View Purpose & Change Lifecycle
The Changes View (`SCR-PORTAL-DELTAS`) tracks **Change Proposals** across their lifecycle through an interactive Kanban board and timeline using humanized statuses (`Proposed`, `In Review`, `Approved`, `Applied`, `Rejected`, `Archived`) and humanized change kinds (`Feature`, `Improvement`, `Bug Fix`):
* **Proposed**: Newly formulated change proposals awaiting review.
* **In Review**: Proposals undergoing review by stakeholders or peer agents.
* **Approved**: Verified proposals ready for implementation.
* **Applied**: Completed proposals whose changes have been integrated into living documentation.
* **Rejected**: Withdrawn or declined proposals preserved for historical auditability.
* **Archived**: Historical proposals grouped into a dedicated paginated column (`Show More Changes`) and status filter pill while preserving their humanized outcome badges.
* **Dynamic Workflow States**: Dynamically surfaces any custom organizational status tag in humanized Title Case.
* **Chronological Timeline (Default) & Kanban Board**: Defaults to a clean, de-carded chronological **Timeline** list with status/kind pills, 1-line summaries, and change counts, paired with a one-click **Board** Kanban toggle and instant search (`"Filter changes by title, summary, or status..."`) that keeps canonical IDs searchable internally without displaying raw slugs.
* **Detailed Specification Inspection**: Clicking any proposal opens a de-carded detail drawer leading with the motivation, collapsible native `<details>` sections for Planned Changes and Affected Areas, `"Open in Full Reader"` escalation, and the full borderless rendered AsciiDoc body (without raw VCS shell snippets or raw JSON `expect:` dumps).
