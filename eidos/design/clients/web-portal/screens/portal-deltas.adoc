== 1. View Purpose & Change Lifecycle
The SpecDeltas View (`SCR-PORTAL-DELTAS`) tracks the atomic lifecycle of architectural mutations through an interactive Kanban board:
* **Proposed**: Newly formulated change requests awaiting architectural review.
* **In Review**: Deltas undergoing review by Human-in-the-Loop or peer agents.
* **Approved**: Verified deltas ready for implementation.
* **Applied**: Merged and reconciled deltas whose mutations have been integrated into living specs.
