== 1. View Purpose & Business Context
The Business Architecture View (`SCR-PORTAL-BUSINESS`) leads the product-engineering conversation using clean human-readable titles, humanized badges, and plain-language labels (never rendering raw ID slugs, literal backticks, or raw test file paths):
* **Domains**: Core areas of the product, responsibilities, and humanized classification badges.
* **Capabilities**: Customer outcomes, ownership, and clickable resolved links to bound domains, containers, interface contracts, personas, and policies.
* **Policies**: Organization-wide rules and compliance constraints with humanized category and severity badges (`Mandatory`, `Warning`, `Advisory`).
* **Personas**: Human and automated actors with humanized archetype badges and resolved capability links.
* **System Rules & Automated Verification**: Non-negotiable rules presented with humanized rule titles, resolved owning entity titles, and plain `Test Verified` indicators.
