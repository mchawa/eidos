= Screen: Business Architecture & Governance View
:toc: left

== 1. View Purpose & Business Context
The Business Architecture View (`SCR-PORTAL-BUSINESS`) leads the product-engineering conversation with a clean, de-carded editorial layout using human-readable titles, humanized badges, and plain-language labels (never rendering raw ID slugs, literal backticks, or raw test file paths):
* **Domains & Capabilities**: Divided domain sections with mission statements and indented clickable capability rows (eliminating nested cards).
* **Policies**: Scannable divided rows presenting organization-wide compliance constraints with humanized category and severity badges (`Mandatory`, `Warning`, `Advisory`).
* **Personas**: Clean divided stakeholder rows listing human and automated actors with archetype badges, roles, and key goals.
* **System Rules & Automated Verification**: Semantic responsive sticky-header table (`#businessRulesTable` inside a keyboard-scrollable `.table-wrapper`) listing verified rules, guarantees, owning sources, and `Test Verified` status.
* **De-Carded Drawers**: Inspection drawers collapse verbose structured metadata into native `<details>` disclosures so full AsciiDoc specification prose leads clearly, paired with `"Open in Full Reader"` escalation.
