:toc: left

== 1. View Purpose & Business Context
The Business Architecture View (`SCR-PORTAL-BUSINESS`) leads the product-engineering conversation by establishing what problems the system solves, how it is governed, and who it serves:
* **Domains (DOM-*)**: Bounded contexts, core responsibilities, and strategic classification (core, supporting, generic).
* **Capabilities (CAP-*)**: Customer Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD), success metrics, and clickable technical capability anchors (`CNT-*` containers and `CTR-*` contracts) plus experience links (`SCR-*`, `FLOW-*`, `UIC-*`).
* **Policies (POL-*)**: System-wide governance policies and compliance constraints.
* **Personas (PER-*)**: Human and AI agent actors operating within the system lifecycle.
* **Invariants Matrix (INV-*)**: Non-negotiable executable invariant rules and automated test bindings.
