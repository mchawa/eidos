== 1. View Purpose & Business Context
The Business Architecture View (`SCR-PORTAL-BUSINESS`) leads the product-engineering conversation by establishing who the system serves and what problems it solves:
* **Personas (PER-*)**: Human and AI agent actors operating within the system lifecycle.
* **Domains (DOM-*)**: Bounded contexts, core responsibilities, and strategic classification (core, supporting, generic).
* **Capabilities (CAP-*)**: Customer Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD), success metrics, and technical capability anchors.
* **Invariants & Policies Matrix (POL-*, INV-*)**: System governance policies and non-negotiable invariant test bindings.
