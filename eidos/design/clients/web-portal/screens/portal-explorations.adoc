= Screen: Technical Explorations & Architectural Spikes View
:toc: left

== 1. View Purpose & Discovery Lifecycle
The **Explorations & Research** View (`SCR-PORTAL-EXPLORATIONS`) tracks hypothesis-driven architectural research and spikes with human-readable titles, humanized status/outcome badges, and resolved links to resulting change proposals (never showing raw ID slugs or raw file paths):
* **Hypotheses & Questions**: Documents core problem statements and research questions under investigation.
* **Empirical Spikes**: Tracks targeted proof-of-concept tasks and research experiments.
* **Exit Criteria & Recommendations**: Enforces explicit time-boxing, hydrated `exit_criteria` checklists, and structured `recommendation` summaries extracted from exploration specifications.
* **Decisions & Learnings**: Displays humanized outcome badges (`Adopted`, `Discarded`, `Proposed`, `In Progress`) and empirical takeaways, plus an `Archived` status filter pill and badge for archived explorations.
* **Resulting Change Proposal Bridge**: 1-click navigation into resulting change proposals displaying each proposal's human-readable title and clean `"Open <Change Title>"` tooltip.
* **Scannable List & Detailed AsciiDoc Inspection**: Renders explorations in a clean, de-carded scannable list with status badges, hypothesis previews, empirical takeaways, and clickable resulting change links; clicking any row opens a de-carded detail drawer with collapsible `<details>` success criteria, `"Open in Full Reader"` escalation, and full borderless rendered AsciiDoc rationale.
