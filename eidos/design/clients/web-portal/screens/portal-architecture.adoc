= Screen: Technical Architecture & C4 Topologies View
:toc: left

== 1. View Purpose & Engineering Scope
The Technical Architecture View (`SCR-PORTAL-ARCHITECTURE`) visualizes the technical runtime structures implementing the system's capabilities using human-readable titles, plain-language labels, and slug-free Mermaid diagrams:
* **Hierarchical Architecture Explorer**: Dynamic clickable zoom across **System Context**, **Containers**, and **Components** (preserving internal `#c4LevelSwitcher`, `#btnC4Level1`–`3`, `#c4Breadcrumbs`, `#c4ContainerSelect`, `#c4DiagramContainer`, `#c4ActiveDiagram`, and `#c4InspectorDrawer` hooks) with pan-drag click suppression and stable component-ID-derived Mermaid node IDs (`mod_<CMP_SLUG>`) for exact click and blast-radius binding.
* **Interactive Breadcrumbs & Navigation**: Seamless drill-down on click with human-readable tooltips, ancestor navigation trail, container quick-switcher dropdown displaying container titles, and level-up controls.
* **Component Inspector Drawer**: Slide-over details drawer revealing module responsibilities, resolved upstream/downstream container and external service titles, resolved contract bindings, uncramped full-height **Full Specification** AsciiDoc prose (`measure="full"`, zero nested scroll boxes) for both Containers (`CNT-*`) and focused internal Components (`CMP-*`), a one-click **"Open in Full Reader"** escalation action launching `SpecReader` with sticky Table of Contents, and automatic drawer dismissal when drilling down into internal component diagrams.
* **Containers & Components Matrix**: Container and internal module cards leading with human-readable titles, humanized container types (`Web App`, `CLI App`, `Library`), and one-click `"Explore Internal Components"` drill-down.
* **Interactive Blast Radius Simulator & SVG Highlighting**: Client-side impact analyzer over pre-computed `data.edges` highlighting **Direct Impact** (`data-blast-hop="1"`) and **Indirect Impact** (`data-blast-hop="2"`) nodes directly on the diagram canvas while listing upstream dependencies, downstream dependents, and enforced rules with resolved human titles.
* **Boundary Interface Contracts & Architectural Decisions**: Interactive contract and decision explorers surfacing resolved provider/consumer container titles, humanized protocol badges (`HTTP JSON`, `CLI`, `Python API`), and full AsciiDoc readers.
