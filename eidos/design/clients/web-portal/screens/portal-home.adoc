= Screen: Portal Executive Dashboard & Home View
:toc: left

== 1. View Layout & Visual Hierarchy
The Home View (`SCR-PORTAL-HOME`) serves as the central landing page and executive dashboard of the Eidos Living Web Portal:
* **System Archetype Banner**: Summarizes the system identity (`SYS-EIDOS-001`), root governance rules, and specification validation health.
* **KPI Metrics Grid**: Surfaces critical system figures at a glance: Total Living Specs, Domains, Capabilities, Invariants, C4 Containers, UI Components, and SpecDeltas.
* **Quick Navigation Hub**: Direct entry points to Business Architecture, Design Systems, Technical Topologies, and SpecDeltas.
* **Validation & Governance Diagnostics**: Collapsible static/live diagnostics drawer surfacing schema validation errors (`schema_errors`), broken cross-references (`broken_references`), and live uncommitted drift status (`/api/drift`) with clickable links to open offending specs.
* **Recent SpecDeltas Feed**: Summarizes active mutations, proposed changes, and recently applied architectural evolutions.
