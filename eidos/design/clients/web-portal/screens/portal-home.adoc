:toc: left

== 1. View Layout & Visual Hierarchy
The Home View (`SCR-PORTAL-HOME`) serves as the central landing page and executive dashboard of the Eidos Living Web Portal with human-readable titles and plain-language labels throughout:
* **System Overview Banner**: Summarizes the system title, plain-language overview, specification validation health (`Up to Date`, `Sync Needed`, and `Live` real-time status), and a clickable amber badge whenever non-blocking advisory validation `warnings` are present, without raw system ID slugs or methodology jargon.
* **KPI Metrics Grid**: Surfaces critical system figures at a glance: Specifications, Domains, Capabilities, System Containers, Active Changes, and Automated Checks.
* **Quick Navigation & How Documentation Stays Current**: Direct entry points to Business & Domains, Architecture, Design Systems, and Changes alongside plain-language guidance cards (Routine Updates, Planned Changes, Living Decisions).
* **Validation & Governance Diagnostics**: Collapsible static/live diagnostics drawer surfacing validation issues, broken links, a collapsible amber advisory `warnings` list, and real-time uncommitted drift with human-readable document and component titles linked directly to the corresponding specification reader.
* **Recent Changes Feed & System Rules**: Summarizes recent change proposals and verified rules using humanized titles and plain `Test Verified` indicators.
