= SpecDelta: Fix Cramped Full Specification Reading Space in Container and Component Inspector
:toc: left

== Problem Statement
In the Living Web Portal's Architecture view (`SCR-PORTAL-ARCHITECTURE`, `src/eidos/portal/frontend/src/components/views/ArchitectureView.tsx`), inspecting a C4 Level 2 Container (`CNT-*`) or a C4 Level 3 Component (`CMP-*`) opens `#c4InspectorDrawer` inside a `95vh` `Sheet` modal (`max-w-[94vw] 2xl:max-w-[1550px]`). However, the **Full Specification** text area is severely cramped for three concrete reasons:

1. **Nested Fixed-Height Scroll Trap (`ArchitectureView.tsx:1553`)**: `<AsciiDocRenderer>` is wrapped in `<div className="max-h-[360px] overflow-y-auto rounded-xl p-3 bg-card border border-border/60">`. Because `Sheet` (`sheet.tsx:107`) already owns vertical scrolling (`flex-1 overflow-y-auto`), users get a tiny 360px inner scroll box inside a 95vh scrollable modal.
2. **Double-Nested Card Borders & Wasted Space (`ArchitectureView.tsx:1553` + `AsciiDocRenderer.tsx:175,181`)**: `<AsciiDocRenderer>` already renders its own outer card (`p-5 sm:p-7 rounded-2xl bg-card border`) and ignores its declared `measure?: "prose" | "full"` prop (hardcoding `max-w-4xl xl:max-w-5xl mx-auto`). Wrapping it inside another bordered `p-3` box wastes both vertical height (leaving ~260px of visible prose) and horizontal width. By contrast, `BusinessView.tsx`, `DeltasView.tsx`, `DesignView.tsx`, and `ExplorationsView.tsx` all render `<AsciiDocRenderer>` directly without any `max-h-[...]` cap or double card.
3. **Missing Focused Component (`CMP-*`) Full Specification & Full-Reader Escalation**: When a user clicks an internal C4 Level 3 Component (`CMP-*`) node—opening `#c4InspectorDrawer` with `focusedSubComponentId`—the drawer only displays the component's short `responsibility` blurb even though `App.tsx` and `generator.py` already hydrate `sub.description` and chunk metadata (`hasSpecBodyOrChunk(sub.id, sub.description)`). On containers with 10+ components, the focused component is also buried down the list, and there is no one-click `"Open in Full Reader"` action to jump into the dedicated full-stage `SpecReader` (with sticky On-This-Page TOC). Note that calling bare `requestSpecNavigation("CNT-..." / "CMP-...")` routes back to `ArchitectureView` in `App.tsx:203-205`, so `specNav.ts` and `App.tsx` must support an explicit `mode: "reader"` target.

== Proposed Specification Changes
* **`CMP-PORTAL-ASSETS` (`src/eidos/portal/frontend/src/components/views/ArchitectureView.tsx`, `src/eidos/portal/frontend/src/components/ui/AsciiDocRenderer.tsx`, `src/eidos/portal/frontend/src/lib/specNav.ts`, `src/eidos/portal/frontend/src/App.tsx`)**:
  * Remove the `max-h-[360px] overflow-y-auto rounded-xl p-3 bg-card border border-border/60` wrapper around `<AsciiDocRenderer>` under **Full Specification** in `#c4InspectorDrawer` (and remove the redundant outer card wrapper around `<AsciiDocRenderer>` in the contract inspector sheet at `ArchitectureView.tsx:1622`) so prose flows naturally inside the scrollable `Sheet` viewport with zero nested scrollbars and generous reading space.
  * Honor the existing `measure?: "prose" | "full"` prop in `AsciiDocRenderer.tsx` (defaulting to `"prose"` `w-full max-w-4xl xl:max-w-5xl mx-auto` to preserve `INV-PORTAL-READING-ERGONOMICS`, and supporting `measure="full"` `w-full` when full drawer width is desired) while preserving `.prose-reading`, `.prose-measure`, and `.spec-heading` typography rules.
  * Extend `SpecNavDetail` and `requestSpecNavigation(target, options?: { mode?: "entity" | "reader" })` in `specNav.ts` and handle `detail.mode === "reader"` in `App.tsx` (`setFocusSpec({ id, nonce }); setActiveTab("catalog", id);`) so `"Open in Full Reader"` reliably opens the full-stage `SpecReader` with sticky Table of Contents for any `CNT-*` or `CMP-*` specification instead of looping back into `#c4InspectorDrawer`.
  * Surface uncramped **Full Specification** prose for **both** Containers (`CNT-*`) and Components (`CMP-*`):
    * When an internal component (`CMP-*`) is focused (`focusedSubComponentId`), surface a dedicated focused-component **Full Specification** section near the top of `#c4InspectorDrawer` (or auto-scroll directly to it and expand its full AsciiDoc body) using an uncapped `<AsciiDocRenderer measure="full" specId={sub.id} content={sub.description || ""} />` with human-readable title (`INV-PORTAL-HUMAN-READABLE-UI`) and an **"Open in Full Reader"** button (`requestSpecNavigation(sub.id, { mode: "reader" })`).
    * Allow any internal component card in `#c4InspectorDrawer` with `hasSpecBodyOrChunk(sub.id, sub.description)` to expand its uncapped **Full Specification** inline or launch **"Open in Full Reader"**.
    * Add an **"Open in Full Reader"** button in the Container **Full Specification** header (`requestSpecNavigation(inspectedComponent.id, { mode: "reader" })`).
  * Preserve `#c4InspectorDrawer` DOM hook (exercised by `tests/test_portal_spike.py`), `INV-PORTAL-HUMAN-READABLE-UI` (zero raw Eidos ID slugs in visible chrome), and `INV-PORTAL-AIR-GAPPED-BUNDLE` (`<= 5 MiB` uncompressed bundle, zero remote subresources).
* **`UIC-PORTAL-READER` (`eidos/design/systems/core/components/portal-reader.adoc`)**:
  * Graduate `DEC-PORTAL-UNCRAMPED-INSPECTOR-PROSE` into `UIC-PORTAL-READER`'s `decisions:` block and document embedded reading-stage guardrails: inspector drawers embedding `AsciiDocRenderer` must never wrap specification prose in fixed-height nested scroll boxes (`max-h-[360px]`) or double-nested card frames, and must expose an `"Open in Full Reader"` escalation action into `SpecReader`.
* **`SCR-PORTAL-ARCHITECTURE` (`eidos/design/clients/web-portal/screens/portal-architecture.adoc`)**:
  * Update the Component Inspector Drawer section to document full-height, uncramped **Full Specification** prose rendering for both Containers (`CNT-*`) and focused internal Components (`CMP-*`), plus `"Open in Full Reader"` navigation into `SpecReader`.
* **`CAP-EXP-001` (`eidos/domains/experience-portal/capabilities/living-web-portal.adoc`) & `INV-PORTAL-INSPECTOR-FULL-SPEC-READING`**:
  * Add `INV-PORTAL-INSPECTOR-FULL-SPEC-READING` under `CAP-EXP-001` (`technical.invariants`, bumping `CAP-EXP-001` `version` per SemVer on apply) bound to `tests/test_portal.py::test_portal_inspector_full_specification_ergonomics`.
