= SpecDelta: Streamline v1 Specification Schemas, Move Prose to AsciiDoc Body, and Derive Component Dependencies via AST
:toc: left

== Problem Statement

Although `DLT-2026-091` canonicalized field names across the 16 Eidos v1 schemas (`assets/schemas/v1/`), several schemas still force narrative prose into YAML frontmatter, maintain unused or vendor-locked sub-objects, require manual component dependency lists that duplicate AST analysis, or impose overly restrictive constraints on SpecDelta mutations:

1. **Redundant `schema` Field in Frontmatter (#1)**: All 16 schemas declare `schema: schemas/v1/...` in YAML frontmatter even though `SpecValidator.resolve_schema()` deterministically resolves the schema 1:1 from `type` via `TYPE_TO_SCHEMA_NAME`.
2. **Bespoke & Unvalidated `quality_attributes` in Frontmatter (#2)**: `#/$defs/quality_attributes` defines 6 fixed fields (including Eidos-specific `max_validation_latency_ms`) that are never verified by `SpecValidator` or `DriftDetector` and belong in AsciiDoc prose.
3. **Manual Component `interfaces` (`provides` & `consumes`) (#3)**: `#/$defs/in_process_interfaces` asks authors to hand-maintain exported signatures (`provides`) and peer component calls (`consumes`) in YAML even though `AstGrepIndexer` and `_CodeOwnershipResolver` already map every component's `source_path` and can derive cross-component consumption directly from AST imports.
4. **Vendor-Locked `external_references` (#5)**: `#/$defs/external_design_references` restricts keys to `figma_url` and `storybook_url` (`additionalProperties: false`), rejecting other design and documentation tools.
5. **Prose-in-YAML Across Product & Architecture Schemas (#7, #8, #9, #10, #11, #12, #13)**:
   * `product/system.schema.yaml` (#7) requires `business_identity` (`vision`, `target_users`, `value_proposition`) and `technical_profile` (`primary_languages`, `runtimes_and_frameworks`, `deployment_targets`, `vcs_platforms`, `agent_support`), none of which are read by the engine or portal.
   * `product/domain.schema.yaml` (#8) requires `boundaries` (`responsibilities`, `out_of_scope`) and defines unused `ubiquitous_language` in YAML instead of AsciiDoc prose.
   * `product/capability.schema.yaml` (#9) requires narrative `business.jtbd` in YAML and defines unused `business.pricing_tiers`.
   * `product/persona.schema.yaml` (#10) requires `goals` and `pain_points` (plus `interaction_modes`) in YAML instead of AsciiDoc prose.
   * `product/exploration.schema.yaml` (#11) encodes the entire spike narrative (`hypothesis`, `questions_to_answer`, `spikes_to_run`, `exit_criteria`, `decision.learnings`) in YAML and restricts `decision.resulting_delta_id` to a single string instead of plural `resulting_deltas`.
   * `tech/architecture.schema.yaml` (#12, #13) requires `topology_style`, `solution_strategy`, `cross_cutting_concepts`, and `deployment_view` (`hosting_model`, `ci_cd_engine`)—all unread by the engine—and duplicates `CNT-*` definitions inside `c4_containers`.
6. **Mandatory `specification_file` on Contracts (#15)**: `tech/contract.schema.yaml` requires `specification_file`, preventing conceptual, external, or inline AsciiDoc contracts.
7. **Bespoke `test_bindings` and `accessibility` on UI Components (#18)**: `design/ui-component.schema.yaml` uses a separate 3-key `test_bindings` object and `accessibility` block instead of standard `invariants` (`INV-*` with `test_binding`).
8. **Unused `vcs` Keys & Overly Restrictive Mutation Rules in `delta.schema.yaml` (#20, #21)**:
   * `vcs.pr_url` and `vcs.applied_at_commit` (#20) are never read or written anywhere in Eidos.
   * `mutations[].target` and `parent` omit `SYS-*`; `operation: deprecate` is artificially restricted to `CAP-*` and `POL-*` despite all 14 living schemas supporting `status: deprecated`; `ARCH-*` forbids `operation: add`; and `mutations[].expect.frontmatter` forces non-hardcoded frontmatter keys into a nested `.custom` wrapper (#21).
