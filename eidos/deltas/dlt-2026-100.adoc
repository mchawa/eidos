== Summary & Motivation
Simplifies `eidos/.cache/index.db` (`SCHEMA_VERSION = "2.10.0"`) by removing 6 dead/duplicate columns across 4 tables, eliminating AST symbol duplication between `code_symbols` and `symbol_mappings`, dropping 6 synthetic reverse edge types from `edges`, and replacing all remaining stdlib `json` serialization/deserialization calls across `src/eidos/` and `tests/` with `orjson`.
