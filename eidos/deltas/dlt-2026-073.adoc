= SpecDelta: Unified v1 JSON Schema Parity for Container & Policy Invariants, Cross-Layer Embedded Decisions, Contract Versioning, and SpecDelta Design References
:toc: left

== Problem Statement

Six bundled v1 JSON Schemas under `assets/schemas/v1/` enforce `additionalProperties: false` while omitting properties that are actively indexed, sliced, or verified by the Eidos engine (`sync.py`, `context.py`, `invariants.py`, `delta_engine.py`, `structural.py`):

1. **`container.schema.yaml` Forbids Top-Level and Inline Component `invariants` (`assets/schemas/v1/tech/container.schema.yaml`, lines 73-110 & 210-232)**:
   * `sync.py`, `context.py`, and `invariants.py` extract `invariants` from `tech/container` and inline `components[]`, and `SpecValidator` injects `$defs.invariant` into `container.schema.yaml`, but `container.schema.yaml` never declares `invariants` in top-level `properties` or inline `components[].properties`.
2. **`policy.schema.yaml` Forbids `invariants` and `decisions` (`assets/schemas/v1/product/policy.schema.yaml`, lines 5-82)**:
   * `invariants.py`, `sync.py`, and `delta_engine.py` (`add_invariant` / `patch_invariant`) support `invariants` and `decisions` on `product/policy` specs, but `policy.schema.yaml` rejects both keys.
3. **`domain.schema.yaml` and `capability.schema.yaml` Forbid `decisions` (and `ubiquitous_language` / `owners` on Domains) (`assets/schemas/v1/product/domain.schema.yaml`, lines 5-119 & `assets/schemas/v1/product/capability.schema.yaml`, lines 5-286)**:
   * `sync.py` (`_upsert_decisions`) and `eidos/SKILL.md` support embedded `DEC-*` architectural decisions on `DOM-*` and `CAP-*` specs, and `eidos-spec/SKILL.md` documents `ubiquitous_language` and `owners` on `DOM-*` specs, yet both schemas reject those keys.
4. **`contract.schema.yaml` Forbids `version`, `consumers`, and `decisions` (`assets/schemas/v1/tech/contract.schema.yaml`, lines 5-69)**:
   * `delta_engine.py` (`modify_contract` with `expect: { version: "1.2.0" }`), `eidos/SKILL.md`, `eidos-plan/SKILL.md`, and `eidos-spec/SKILL.md` rely on SemVer `version`, `consumers`, and `decisions` on `CTR-*` contracts, which `contract.schema.yaml` currently forbids.
5. **`delta.schema.yaml` `affects` Omits Design/Persona Keys and `blast_radius` Omits `x-eidos-ref` (`assets/schemas/v1/delta/delta.schema.yaml`, lines 250-368)**:
   * `structural.py` (`_collect_claimed_entities`) reads `affects.{design_systems,clients,flows,screens,ui_components}`, and `delta.schema.yaml` `mutations[].action` supports `add_design_system`, `modify_screen`, `add_persona`, etc., yet `affects` rejects those keys.
   * `blast_radius.impacted_components` and `blast_radius.impacted_containers` lack `x-eidos-ref` metadata, so `SpecValidator._validate_references` never verifies that impacted component/container IDs exist in the catalog.

== Proposed Specification Changes

1. **Allow `invariants` and `decisions` Across Owning Schemas (`container.schema.yaml`, `policy.schema.yaml`, `domain.schema.yaml`, `capability.schema.yaml`, `contract.schema.yaml`)**:
   * Add `invariants` (`$ref: "#/$defs/invariant"`) to `container.schema.yaml` (top-level and inline `components[]`) and `policy.schema.yaml`.
   * Add `decisions` (`$ref: "#/$defs/decision"`) to `domain.schema.yaml`, `capability.schema.yaml`, `policy.schema.yaml`, and `contract.schema.yaml`.
   * Add optional `ubiquitous_language` and `owners` to `domain.schema.yaml`, and optional `version` and `consumers` (with `x-eidos-ref` targeting `CNT-*` / `CMP-*`) to `contract.schema.yaml`.
2. **Complete `affects` and `blast_radius` Reference Validation in `delta.schema.yaml`**:
   * Add `design_systems`, `clients`, `flows`, `screens`, `ui_components`, and `personas` (with `x-eidos-ref`) to `affects.properties`.
   * Add `x-eidos-ref` metadata to `blast_radius.impacted_components` (`tech/component`, `CMP-`) and `blast_radius.impacted_containers` (`tech/container`, `CNT-`).
