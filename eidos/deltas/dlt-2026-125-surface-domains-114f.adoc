= SpecDelta: Surface Domains, Personas, Policies, and Design Subgraphs in Context Slices, Drift Gates, and Agent Skills
:toc: left

== Problem Statement
Autonomous coding agents operating in Eidos-governed repositories reliably update C4 Containers (`CNT-*`), Components (`CMP-*`), Contracts (`CTR-*`), and Invariants (`INV-*`), but frequently forget to update **Bounded Context Domains (`DOM-*`)**, **Actor Personas (`PER-*`)**, **Governance Policies (`POL-*`)**, and **Design Specifications (`DS-*`, `UIC-*`, `CLT-*`, `SCR-*`, `FLOW-*`)**.

Root-cause analysis across the context slicer, drift detector, and bundled skills identified four reinforcing blind spots:

1. **`eidos context` Strips Domains, Personas, Design Bindings, and File Paths (`CMP-TIERED-CONTEXT-COMPRESSOR`, `CMP-FTS-QUERY-ENGINE`)**:
   * `AGENTS.md` and `eidos/SKILL.md` instruct agents to run `eidos context -f <file> --json` and *"trust its output directly without re-reading the summarized `.adoc` files"*.
   * However, `TieredContextSlice.to_dict()` only projects five keys (`components`, `contracts`, `capabilities`, `invariants`, `policies`) and strips:
     ** `file_path`, `business.personas`, `business.policies`, and `experience` (`clients`, `design_systems`, `screens`, `flows`, `ui_components`) from `_project_capability()`;
     ** `file_path` and `category` from `_project_policy()`;
     ** the owning `DOM-*` file path (`domain_path`) and bound `CLT-*`/`DS-*` design entities from `_project_component()`.
   * `TASK_SLICE_ENTITY_TYPES` in `src/eidos/core/index/query.py` excludes `product/persona` (`PER-*`), `design/client` (`CLT-*`), `design/ui-component` (`UIC-*`), `design/screen` (`SCR-*`), and `design/flow` (`FLOW-*`), so `eidos context -t "<task>"` drops direct hits on personas, screens, flows, and UI components before expansion.
2. **`eidos drift` Lets `CNT-*`/`CMP-*` Edits Satisfy `ui_contract` Design Drift and Misses `CMP-*` -> `CNT-*` Design Bindings (`CMP-DRIFT-DETECTOR`)**:
   * In `src/eidos/vcs/drift.py` (lines 520–522), when `classify_design_changes()` classifies a frontend edit as `cat == "ui_contract"` (new/deleted `.tsx`/`.vue`/`.svelte` file, exported UI component, `*Props` contract, or route change), `detect_drift` adds `owner_specs_by_file` (`CNT-*`/`CMP-*`) to `satisfying_paths`. As a result, an agent that updates its `CMP-*` spec satisfies `design_drift` without touching `UIC-*`, `SCR-*`, `FLOW-*`, or `CLT-*`.
   * In `src/eidos/vcs/structural.py` (`_resolve_bound_design_ids`), when a frontend file maps to a standalone C4 Level 3 `CMP-*` component, `_resolve_bound_design_ids` only inspects 1-hop neighbors of `CMP-*`. Because `CLT-*` binds to `container: CNT-*` (or via `CAP-*.experience`), files owned by a `CMP-*` whose `CNT-*` parent was not directly matched fail to resolve their bound `CLT-*`/`DS-*` subgraph and silently skip `design_drift`.
3. **Drift Remediation Prompts Point Only to `CMP-*`/`CNT-*` (`src/eidos/vcs/report.py`)**:
   * `build_drift_remediation_and_summary` outputs `• '<file>' relates to CMP-X -> Update 'eidos/architecture/components/...' (or a 1-hop linked capability/contract)`, never reminding the agent of bound `DOM-*`, `PER-*`, `POL-*`, or `design/*` specifications.
4. **Bundled Skills Omit `DOM-*`, `PER-*`, `POL-*`, and `design/*` from Co-Evolution & Self-Review Checklists (`CNT-EIDOS-PLUGIN`)**:
   * `eidos/SKILL.md` §4 (Workflow A, Step 3 "Co-Evolve Living Specs on Structural Changes") lists five rules focused exclusively on `CNT-*`, `CMP-*`, `CTR-*`, `CAP-*`, and `INV-*`/`DEC-*`.
   * `eidos/SKILL.md` §5 (Stage 2 "Mandatory Autonomous Adversarial Gap Review") audits Requirements, Edge Cases, `CTR-*`, `INV-*`, and `DEC-*`, omitting `DOM-*`, `PER-*`, `POL-*`, and `design/*`.

== Proposed Specification Changes

=== 1. Surface Governance & Design Anchors in `eidos context` (`CMP-TIERED-CONTEXT-COMPRESSOR`, `CMP-FTS-QUERY-ENGINE`, `CAP-AGENT-001`)
* **Capability & Policy Projection (`src/eidos/core/index/context.py`)**:
  * In `_project_capability()`: for `guardrails` and `deep` tiers, include `file_path` and (when `not self._compact_prose`) `personas` (from `business.personas`), `policies` (from `business.policies`), and non-empty `experience` lists (`clients`, `design_systems`, `screens`, `flows`, `ui_components`).
  * In `_project_policy()`: include `category` (all non-anchor tiers) and `file_path` (in `guardrails` and `deep` tiers) alongside `id`, `title`, and `severity`.
  * In `_project_component()`: for `guardrails` and `deep` tiers (when `not self._compact_prose`), include `domain_path` (resolved `file_path` of the container/component's `DOM-*` spec) and `design` (bound `CLT-*` / `DS-*` IDs) when present, and render them compactly in `ArchitecturalContextSlice.to_markdown()`.
  * In `_compress_verbose_fields_in_place()` and `_item_md_and_json_upper_bounds()`: account for the new fields and strip optional `personas`/`experience`/`domain_path`/`design` metadata under token-budget pressure before evicting any direct entity matches, preserving both `anchor <= 300`, `guardrails <= 1000`, and `deep <= 2500` token ceilings (`INV-CONTEXT-TOKEN-COMPRESSION`) and the `>= 60.0%` guardrails token-savings floor (`test_spike_c_tiered_context_compression`).
* **Task-Prompt Retrieval Expansion Without Top-K Displacement (`src/eidos/core/index/query.py`, `src/eidos/core/index/context.py`)**:
  * Add `product/persona`, `design/client`, `design/ui-component`, `design/screen`, and `design/flow` to `TASK_SLICE_ENTITY_TYPES`.
  * To prevent `PER-*`, `SCR-*`, `UIC-*`, `FLOW-*`, or `CLT-*` hits from displacing direct `CNT-*`, `CMP-*`, `CTR-*`, `CAP-*`, `POL-*`, `INV-*`, `DOM-*`, or `DS-*` matches from the primary top-8 FTS/Hybrid candidate slots, preserve the primary top-8 candidate allocation for core sliceable types while expanding matched `PER-*`, `CLT-*`, `UIC-*`, `SCR-*`, and `FLOW-*` entities via canonical knowledge-graph edges (`serves_persona`, `delivered_by`, `implemented_by`, `has_screen`, `has_flow`, `styled_by`, and `styles_container`) into `expanded_cnt_ids` and `matched_capabilities`, recording the matched ID in `matched_reasons`.

=== 2. Tighten Design Subgraph Drift & Remediation Guidance (`CMP-DRIFT-DETECTOR`, `CNT-EIDOS-VCS`, `CAP-VCS-001`)
* **Parent Container & Capability Experience Traversal (`src/eidos/vcs/structural.py`)**:
  * Update `_resolve_bound_design_ids(idx, cf, owner_ids)` so that in addition to `find_entities_by_symbol(cf)` (`inferred_path`) and 1-hop neighbors of `owner_ids`, it walks 2-hop paths from `CMP-*` -> parent `CNT-*` (`part_of` / `contains_component`) -> `CLT-*`/`DS-*` (`implemented_by` / `styled_by` / `styles_container`) as well as 1-hop `CAP-*` (`implements`) -> `CLT-*`/`DS-*` (`delivered_by` / `experience.clients` / `experience.design_systems`). This ensures newly added/unindexed frontend files under a `CMP-*` directory and capabilities bound via `experience` always resolve their governing `CLT-*`/`DS-*` subgraph.
* **Require Design Subgraph Co-Evolution for `ui_contract` Changes (`src/eidos/vcs/drift.py`, `DEC-VCS-STRICT-UI-CONTRACT-DESIGN-DRIFT`)**:
  * Remove the `if cat == "ui_contract": satisfying_paths |= owner_specs_by_file...` bypass in `detect_drift()`. Both `token` and `ui_contract` changes on files belonging to a container with a bound `CLT-*` or `DS-*` subgraph must substantively update at least one spec in that design subgraph (`tokens.json`, `DS-*`, `UIC-*`, `CLT-*`, `SCR-*`, or `FLOW-*`) or be declared in an active SpecDelta.
* **Actionable Remediation Suggestions (`src/eidos/vcs/report.py`)**:
  * Enhance `build_drift_remediation_and_summary` so component and design drift bullets remind the agent to co-evolve bound `DOM-*`, `CAP-*` (`personas` / `policies`), and `design/*` (`UIC-*`, `SCR-*`, `FLOW-*`, `CLT-*`, `DS-*`) specifications when structural/UI contracts change.

=== 3. Co-Evolution & Self-Review Checklists in Bundled Skills (`CNT-EIDOS-PLUGIN`)
* **`assets/plugin_reference/skills/eidos/SKILL.md`** (in-place edit within the `2,050`-word ceiling of `INV-PLUGIN-SKILL-SIZE-CEILING`; current body is `2,011` words, leaving `39` words of headroom—tighten surrounding phrasing in place):
  * In **§4 Workflow A, Step 3 ("Co-Evolve Living Specs on Structural Changes")**, add concise bullets for:
    ** **Design token, UI component, screen/route, or user flow change**: Update the bound `design/*` spec (`tokens.json`, `DS-*`, `UIC-*`, `CLT-*`, `SCR-*`, `FLOW-*`)—`CMP-*`/`CNT-*` edits alone do not satisfy `design_drift`.
    ** **Domain concept, actor role, or cross-cutting policy change**: Update ubiquitous language in `DOM-*`, actor definitions/links in `PER-*` / `CAP-*.business.personas`, or governance rules in `POL-*` / `CAP-*.business.policies`.
  * In **§5 Stage 2 ("Mandatory Autonomous Adversarial Gap Review")**, add a 6th checklist item:
    ** **6. Domain, Persona, Policy & Design Parity (`DOM-*`, `PER-*`, `POL-*`, `design/*`)**: Did new domain terms, user roles, cross-cutting rules, or UI tokens/components/screens/flows get reflected in `eidos/domains/`, `eidos/personas/`, `eidos/policies/`, and `eidos/design/`?
* **`assets/plugin_reference/skills/eidos-plan/SKILL.md`** (in-place edit within the `1,650`-word ceiling; current body is `1,605` words, leaving `45` words of headroom):
  * In **§3 ("Staff Systems Architect 5-Dimension Review")**, explicitly include `DOM-*` ubiquitous language, `PER-*` persona links, and `design/*` (`DS-*`, `UIC-*`, `CLT-*`, `SCR-*`, `FLOW-*`) parity alongside `POL-*` and `INV-*`.
* **`assets/plugin_reference/agents-md/block.md`** (within the `250`-word template ceiling and `320`-word rendered ceiling):
  * Mention `domains`, `policies`, and `design` in the `eidos context` bullet so the always-loaded `AGENTS.md` block primes agents on all specification layers.
